# Design and UX review, 25 Oct 2026

Scope: the booth demo. It is shown on a laptop or a TV from two or three metres, so it has to read at a glance, and Simulate has to
carry it when there are no insoles. Design, animation and UX only: no change to flight time, contact edges, ZUPT, thresholds or the
Combine rules; the page's measurement code (`app.js`, `units.js`) is untouched.

**Art direction.** A combine measuring tower for the glasses age. A deep navy instrument panel, condensed scoreboard numerals big enough
to read across a room, mono instrument labels with hairline rules (Teenage Engineering), a strict grid (Muller-Brockmann), and one
thing to remember it by: the **height tower**, a ruler in your unit with your best flagged in amber and your leap rising up it.

## Baseline (before)

83 unit tests and 63 smoke checks green. Screens shot at 1280, 1920 (TV), 390 and 320, light and dark (`shots/athletic-explosiveness-demo/before-*`).

## Findings, ranked by impact

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Not readable from across a room.** The stage was a 40 rem phone column pinned top-left on every screen; on a 1920 px TV about two thirds of the screen was empty and the biggest type was 52 px. | Fixed: booth scale (the whole UI grows with the screen, about 1.4x at 1080p), a two-column stage on wide screens, display tokens (title, prompt, number, stamp) that grow at 40 and 60 rem. |
| 2 | **First screen showed no bests and no next drill.** A returning athlete saw the same headline as a first-timer and a one-line "Last Card". | Fixed: headline and one big button, the next drill (what to beat, the foot to chase), the tower with the best flagged, and all five stations with the athlete's best at each. A first-timer sees the same layout with empty marks. |
| 3 | **Results vanished or lingered wrongly.** A finished station's result was replaced at once by the next station, and the previous result sat under the next prompt. | Fixed: the result stays up 3.2 s (`CAPS.resultDwellMs`), then the next station's "to beat" takes its place; the rail keeps every finished value. |
| 4 | **Section links were a hidden-scrollbar pill carousel** ("Settings" was cut off at 390 px). Breaks the no-sideways-panning rule. | Fixed: quiet mono links that wrap; hidden during a round. |
| 5 | **GO was a small teal block** in a card. | Fixed: the whole panel steps to teal and the word fills it (one steady state, never a flash). |
| 6 | **Simulate looked like the real thing.** Only the Card carried a small "Simulated" tag, and with no insoles the page still led with Start. | Fixed: with no insoles Simulate is the main button and the insoles chip says so; a dashed periwinkle "Simulated demo" bar shows for the whole round. |
| 7 | **Type and colour were generic** (system sans for everything, personal best in white, bright accents as text). | Fixed: a three-voice pairing (condensed display, system sans, mono labels), colour roles from the house style (measured teal, best amber, simulated periwinkle dashed), a deeper navy, a warm-paper light theme, AA contrast table below. |
| 8 | **Phone header ate 300 px** before the stage. | Fixed: a two-up control grid, the brand tag hidden below 30 rem, one-line section links (hidden in a round). |
| 9 | **Lab details overflowed at 320 px** (361 px wide). | Fixed: the page clips sideways overflow, grids shrink (`minmax(0, 1fr)`), the lab tables use their own scroller. |

## What changed

- **Look.** `tokens.css` holds every raw value: three font stacks, the booth scale, display sizes per breakpoint, the tower sizes, both
  palettes. `fun.css` is rewritten around them (tokens only, a reduced-motion twin for every animation, nothing loops or flashes).
- **First screen** (`renderIdle`): headline, lede, safety note, Start and Simulate, the next drill, the insoles chip (live: it updates
  when insoles connect), the tower, the five-station ledger. Pure data in `config.js` (`idleView`, `towerScale`, `flagOffsets`, `railValues`).
- **Stage** (`renderRun`): a five-icon rail with each finished value, a prompt panel, a result panel (number, judgment, tower or ghost bar),
  "to beat" and an invitation to set a first mark before a result exists.
- **Card:** five stamps in a row on a wide screen (rows on a phone), numerals with small units, quiet "Not run".
- **Five custom pictograms** (leap, one foot, first step, sprint, bounce) and an arrow, drawn as stroke-only SVG.

## Contrast (WCAG AA, 4.5:1 for text)

Dark: text 14.9, second text 11.3, muted 7.5 on panel; teal ink 8.5; amber 10.5; periwinkle 8.5. Light: text 18.5, second text 10.6,
muted 6.6 on white; teal ink `#006f5b` 5.1 on the tinted chips (the house ink `#007c66` fell to 4.0 there, so it is a shade deeper);
amber ink 5.9 on its tint; periwinkle ink 6.5.

## Seen, not fixed

- **Real hardware.** Nothing here was tried with insoles; Simulate and events fed through the page's own bridge were used throughout.
- **Display font.** The condensed face is the platform's own (Avenir Next Condensed on macOS and iOS, Bahnschrift on Windows); other
  systems fall back to Arial Narrow or the system sans. That keeps the demo offline and private; a Google Fonts face (for example Barlow
  Condensed) would make it identical everywhere. Open decision.
- **Lab details** keep the original dashboard layout (tables, polar chart, ground-truth boxes); they take the new tokens and type but were
  not redesigned.
- **Haptics** are still not wired (`TODO[SDK]`); every cue has a picture and a word.
- The kit's toast sits at the top of the screen and can cover the header for a few seconds on the Card.
- **Attract mode** for an idle booth screen is a P2 item in the engagement design, so it was not added.
